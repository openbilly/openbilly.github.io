# My Life Mission
To live freely and with integrity, with a clear conscience before the world and my own life.

## My Commitments

### Belief
- **Believe deeply** that I will succeed, and succeed greatly
- **Stay centered** on my own values. Don't let external people or things control me
- **Lead by character** - let my inner strength and positive energy influence the world around me

### Integrity
- **Keep my promises** - my word is my bond
- **Be careful with commitments** - don't make promises lightly or box myself into corners

### Maturity
- **Be decisive and responsible** while remaining understanding and empathetic

### Contentment
- **Build security from within** - from my values, not from material things
- **Pursue excellence at work**, go with the flow in life
- **Appreciate fine things** but know when enough is enough
- **Believe I already have enough** - contentment brings happiness no wealth can match
- **Focus on effort first** - you reap what you sow
- **Aim for completion**, not perfection

### Humility
- **Keep my jewels hidden** - don't show off or flaunt talents
- **Dress clean and presentable** but avoid vanity
- **Share work that's substantive** - don't create just for showing off

### Mindful Speech
- **Talk less, listen more** - avoid oversharing personal matters
- **Get to the point** - be concise and clear
- **Avoid unnecessary apologies** - they can come across as weak
- **Hold my tongue** - no gossip or idle talk
- **Stay in my lane** - don't comment on what's not my responsibility
- **Speak truth** - when I speak, let it be true and actionable
- **Keep confidences** - hear and forget, see and let go

### Self-Reflection
- **Step back regularly** - view myself from an outsider's perspective

### Honesty
- **Take ownership** - look for causes and solutions within myself first
- **No excuses** - if I'm wrong, I'll admit it and correct it

### Courage
- **Face my fears** - dare to break through my own limits
- **Walk toward challenges** - nothing ventured, nothing gained
- **Live in the present** - move forward boldly
- **Be decisive** - the best time is now
- **Ask for help** - the path is under my feet and in seeking guidance

### Composure
- **Be proactive and adaptable** - strong within, flexible without
- **Improve myself relentlessly**, accept others pragmatically
- **Handle events calmly**: first accept, then deal
- **Prepare without worrying** - work gradually toward inner peace
- **View outcomes wisely**: 
  - Success is a gift
  - Failure is protection
  - Delay means timing isn't right
  - Progress comes with support
- **Stay grounded** - don't let achievements go to my head
- **Think before acting**: 
  - Don't send letters when angry
  - Don't give gifts when overly excited
- **Learn from Zeng Guofan**: Clear mind, adapt to circumstances, don't dwell on future, stay focused in moment, release the past

### Openness
- **Wish well for everyone** - even those I dislike
- **Release hatred** - try not to hold it for anyone
- **Avoid envy** - especially of people close to me
- **Maintain empty-cup mindset** - with books and people
- **Don't jump to conclusions** about people
- **Be kind** - nobody owes me anything; build good connections
- **Find teachers everywhere** - learn from others' strengths
- **Learn from others' flaws** - correct them in myself if present, guard against them if not
- **See the whole person** - everyone has noble and petty sides; acknowledge the good, be mindful of the rest

### Service to Others
- **Help others succeed** - with discipline and genuine care
- **Focus on giving** - especially with my wife
- **Never take credit** for someone else's work

### Wisdom & Fairness
- **Hear all sides** before judging
- **Avoid four pitfalls**: assumptions, insisting on certainty, rigidity, self-centeredness

### Resilience
- **Stay true to my初心 (original intention)**
- **Be persistent** - never give up easily
- **See obstacles as opportunities** to build character and strength
- **Work diligently** - keep moving forward

### Continuous Improvement
- **Break bad habits** - constantly better myself
- **Limit online time**: social media, games, shopping, endless browsing, lowbrow content
- **Maintain regular routine** - practice self-discipline
- **Eat mindfully**: lower carbs, sugar, unhealthy fats; avoid overeating
- **Quit smoking**, drink less
- **Exercise consistently**
- **Read regularly**, keep learning English

### Systems & Order
- **Review regularly**: yearly, monthly, weekly, daily - review, analyze, plan, execute
- **Record and act promptly** on ideas and plans (but recording is a tool, not the goal)
- **Put first things first** - no procrastination
- **Start small**: from easy to hard, from near to far, from now to future

- **Spouse**: My wife is the most important person in my life. We share joys and hardships, walking hand-in-hand with unconditional love and support.
- **Father**: Lead by example. Encourage and help my sons experience life's joys through quality time together.
- **Son**: Never forget my parents' kindness. Be considerate and filial.

### Entrepreneur
- Commit to constant learning and self-improvement
- Achieve my success by helping others succeed
- Build and grow a team, becoming an excellent leader
- Create a respected and sustainable company

### The Improver
- Systematically improve intellectually, physically, and spiritually
- Strive to live in harmony with others, society, the world
- Most importantly: live in harmony with **myself**
